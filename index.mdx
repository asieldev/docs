Welcome to the Achylo Protocol documentation. Achylo is a decentralized peer-to-peer escrow protocol on the **Base blockchain** that enables trustless USDC trading against local fiat currencies across LATAM and emerging markets. Here you will find everything you need — whether you are a trader, a merchant, or a developer building on top of the protocol.

## Get Up and Running in 3 Steps

New to Achylo? Follow these three steps to go from zero to your first trade.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet at achylo.com">
    Visit [achylo.com](https://achylo.com) and click **Connect**. You can sign in with an email address — Achylo will automatically create a self-custody smart account wallet for you. No browser extension required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet with USDC on Base">
    Deposit USDC into your Achylo wallet. You can send USDC directly on the Base network, or deposit from another chain (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, and more) and Achylo will automatically bridge and convert it to USDC on Base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start trading on the P2P marketplace">
    Navigate to **Tools → P2P** in the app. Browse open offers or create your own, select a fiat currency, and let the smart contract escrow handle the rest. Gas fees are sponsored — you only need USDC.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Achylo Protocol is deployed on **Base Mainnet** (chain ID `8453`). A testnet environment is available on **Base Sepolia** for developers and explorers.
</Note>
