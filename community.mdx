<Warning>

If someone claiming to be from Achylo requests your private key or seed phrase, it is a scam. Report it immediately to the support channels listed below.
</Warning>

## How to Participate

Getting involved with the Achylo community is straightforward. Here are four ways to make the most of the ecosystem:

1. **Introduce yourself in the community.** Share your experience level and the countries or regions you operate in. This helps connect you with users who have similar needs and trading preferences.

2. **Follow announcements on X and Telegram.** Fee changes, new protocol features, maintenance windows, and governance proposals are always posted on official channels first. Staying subscribed ensures you never miss a critical update.

3. **Report incidents through official support channels.** If you experience an issue with a transaction or suspect fraudulent activity, contact the team via email at [contact@achylo.com](mailto:contact@achylo.com) or via the [Achylo Labs Telegram](https://t.me/achylolabs). Include screenshots and the relevant transaction hash to speed up the response.

4. **Contribute to documentation improvements.** If you spot an error, a gap, or an opportunity to make these guides clearer, share your suggestions in the official community channels. The Achylo team reviews all feedback actively.

## Community Rules

A healthy community is built on mutual respect and shared responsibility. All members are expected to follow these basic guidelines:

- **Respect all members.** Avoid sharing personal or sensitive information in public channels, and treat others with courtesy regardless of experience level.
- **Verify admin badges before following any instructions.** Official Achylo administrators are identifiable by their verified badges. Do not follow instructions from unverified accounts claiming to represent Achylo.
- **Never share private keys or seed phrases.** This applies everywhere — public channels, direct messages, and email. The Achylo team will never ask for them.
- **Report suspicious activity immediately.** If you observe behavior that seems fraudulent, abusive, or deceptive, report it to the [Telegram Support](https://t.me/achylolabs) or via [contact@achylo.com](mailto:contact@achylo.com).

<Note>
  Staying active in official channels is the best way to receive the latest protocol news, resolve questions quickly, and contribute to Achylo's growth as a community-driven protocol.
</Note>
