## Executive Summary

Achylo is a decentralized peer-to-peer (P2P) escrow protocol built on the **Base blockchain**. It enables secure, transparent, and efficient transactions **without intermediaries** by combining advanced smart-contract architecture with user-friendly interfaces. By using cryptographic guarantees and on-chain logic, Achylo:

- Minimizes counterparty risk across domestic and cross-border transactions.
- Reduces costs to a fraction of what traditional payment processors charge.
- Delivers community-driven dispute resolution that is transparent and auditable.
- Provides global access to anyone with an internet-connected wallet.

## 1. The Problem

Traditional P2P commerce is burdened by structural inefficiencies that harm both buyers and sellers:

| Pain Point | Description |
|---|---|
| **Trust asymmetry** | Parties must either trust each other blindly or rely on costly centralized intermediaries. |
| **High costs** | Intermediaries typically charge **2–5%** per transaction. |
| **Slow settlement** | Legacy payment systems can take days to finalize. |
| **Geographic limitations** | Cross-border deals face regulatory and operational friction. |
| **Opaque disputes** | Centralized arbitration is slow, expensive, and difficult to audit. |

## 2. The Achylo Solution

Achylo addresses these gaps with a protocol designed for trustless commerce at scale:

## 3. Protocol Architecture

Achylo relies on four primary smart contracts working in concert.

### 3.1 P2P Escrow Contract

The core of the protocol. It manages the entire lifecycle of an escrow transaction — from initiation to settlement or dispute resolution.

**Responsibilities:**
- Transaction initiation with explicit, agreed-upon terms.
- Collateral management for merchants posting security deposits.
- Payment method verification and enforcement.
- Secure custody of stablecoins until settlement conditions are met.
- Dispute workflows handled by community arbiters.

**Key capabilities:**

| Capability | Details |
|---|---|
| Multi-state management | `pending` → `confirmed` → `released` → `disputed` |
| Custom payment methods | Configurable exchange rates per transaction |
| Tiered reputation system | New, Active, Merchant, and Gold user tiers |
| Automatic timeouts | Guaranteed finality even without counterparty action |
| Collateral slashing | Fraudulent behavior results in loss of collateral |

### 3.2 CUBT Token Contract

CUBT is Achylo's native utility token that powers the protocol's economic layer.

- **Minting:** Converts USDT into CUBT at a dynamic fee.
- **Redemption:** Converts CUBT back into USDT on demand.
- **Dynamic fees:** Mint and redeem fees adjust to protocol economics.
- **Overcollateralization:** Ensures price stability and system security.
- **Staking integration:** Rewards long-term holders with yield and fee discounts.

### 3.3 Referral System Contract

Incentivizes organic network growth and user acquisition:

- Registers referrers and tracks commissions on-chain.
- Calculates rewards programmatically without manual intervention.
- Applies tier-based bonuses for high-activity referrers.
- Blocks circular or fraudulent referral loops.
- Distributes rewards directly to wallet addresses.

### 3.4 Recovery Module Contract

Provides account safety and optional social recovery mechanisms:

- Guardian registration and management by the account owner.
- Email verification layer for recovery requests.
- Timelock protections that prevent instant account takeovers.
- Secure ownership transfers through multi-step approval.
- Social recovery with community involvement for edge cases.

## 4. Transaction Flow

### 4.1 Standard Escrow

```
Buyer creates escrow → Seller accepts & collateral locked → Buyer sends payment
→ Seller confirms receipt → Seller delivers → Buyer confirms → Funds released
```

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Initiation** — Buyer creates an escrow with defined terms; seller accepts and collateral is locked (if required).
2. **Payment** — Buyer sends payment via the agreed method; seller confirms receipt while funds remain in escrow.
3. **Delivery** — Seller delivers goods or services; buyer confirms delivery.
4. **Settlement** — Smart contract releases funds when conditions are met. The escrow closes on-chain with immutable proof.

### 4.2 Dispute Resolution Flow

When a party believes the transaction terms have not been met, the following process activates:

1. **Dispute Initiation** — Either party raises a dispute; an evidence submission window opens; arbiters are assigned automatically.
2. **Arbitration** — Community arbiters review submitted evidence. A **24–48 hour voting period** determines the outcome by majority vote.
3. **Resolution** — Funds are distributed according to the arbiter decision; the losing party may forfeit collateral; the transaction closes with a fully auditable on-chain record.

## 5. Security Model

### 5.1 Collateral System

- Merchants post collateral proportional to their order capacity.
- **Slashing mechanisms** punish fraudulent behavior by seizing posted collateral.
- Collateral is returned after successful activity, subject to cooldown periods.
- Tiered requirements ensure new merchants ramp up responsibility incrementally.

### 5.2 Rate Limiting

- Tier-based hourly and daily transaction limits mitigate spam and sybil attacks.
- Cooldown periods prevent burst attacks across multiple accounts.
- Critical operations can be exempted by governance when necessary.

### 5.3 Account Freezing

| Trigger | Details |
|---|---|
| Automatic triggers | Fraud signals or repeated dispute losses activate freezing |
| Manual governance freeze | Governance can freeze accounts posing protocol risk |
| Appeal process | Users retain a defined appeal path with clear criteria |
| Auto-unfreeze | Account access restores automatically once conditions are satisfied |

### 5.4 Cryptographic Security

- **Regular third-party audits** of all smart contracts by independent firms.
- **Multi-signature controls** across all treasury operations.
- **Timelocks** enforced before any critical protocol upgrade executes.
- **Governance approval** required for all protocol parameter changes.

## 6. Economic Model

### 6.1 Fee Structure

Achylo generates sustainable revenue via transparent, on-chain fees.

#### Escrow Fees

| Fee Type | Rate |
|---|---|
| Base transaction fee | **0.1%** (governance configurable) |
| Dispute fees | Deducted from losing party's collateral |
| Arbitration rewards | Paid from dispute fee pool |

#### Token Fees

| Operation | Fee Range |
|---|---|
| Mint (USDT → CUBT) | **0.1% – 1%** |
| Redeem (CUBT → USDT) | **0.1% – 1%** |

#### Fee Reductions

| Benefit | Discount |
|---|---|
| CUBT token holding | Up to **50%** off |
| Staking discount | Up to **30%** additional reduction |
| Gold membership | Up to **75%** off |

### 6.2 Fee Distribution

Collected fees flow to four destinations:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Treasury** | Funds protocol operations and development |
| **Arbiters** | Compensation for dispute resolution work |
| **Stakers** | Rewards for securing the network |
| **Referrers** | Commissions on referred transaction volume |

### 6.3 CUBT Tokenomics

- **Demand-backed minting:** CUBT is minted only when users deposit USDT, keeping supply tied to real demand.
- **On-demand redemption:** Users can always redeem CUBT back to USDT.
- **Overcollateralized reserves:** Excess backing guarantees the redemption ratio.
- **Deflationary pressure:** A portion of fees are burned, reducing circulating supply over time.

**CUBT utility:**
- Reduced escrow and token fees.
- Staking rewards and yield generation.
- Governance voting rights.
- Gold-tier access and perks.

## 7. User Tiers and Reputation

### 7.1 Tier System

| Tier | Requirement | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **New User** | 0 completed escrows | Standard limits and fees |
| **Active User** | 1+ completed escrows | Doubled limits, reduced collateral requirements |
| **Merchant** | 10+ completed escrows | Unlimited limits, public order posting, collateral supply |
| **Gold User** | 50+ escrows or paid membership | Unlimited limits, priority disputes, exclusive perks, 75% fee reduction |

### 7.2 Reputation Mechanics

- On-chain transaction history provides full transparency and is publicly verifiable.
- Dispute outcomes (wins and losses) modify a user's standing, future collateral requirements, and tier eligibility.

## 8. Governance and Decentralization

### 8.1 Protocol Governance

Achylo governance is token-weighted:

- **CUBT holders** submit and vote on proposals to adjust protocol parameters.
- Approved changes execute only **after timelock delays** for security.
- Emergency proposals can be fast-tracked with supermajority approval.

### 8.2 Governance-Controlled Parameters

- Escrow and token fee rates.
- Merchant collateral requirements.
- Rate limits per user tier.
- Arbitration and dispute policies.
- Treasury allocations and development budgets.

### 8.3 Decentralization Roadmap

| Phase | Description |
|---|---|
| **Phase 1** | Centralized governance with structured community feedback. |
| **Phase 2** | Token-weighted voting rolls out incrementally for select parameters. |
| **Phase 3** | Transition to a full DAO structure with community-elected delegates. |
| **Phase 4** | Entire protocol configuration managed on-chain by the community. |
