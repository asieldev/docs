The Achylo P2P marketplace lets you trade USDC directly with other people, using local fiat currencies as the payment method. Every trade is protected by a smart contract escrow on **Base blockchain** — your USDC is locked until both parties confirm the transaction, eliminating counterparty risk without relying on a central authority. This guide walks you through accessing the app, connecting your wallet, and understanding what currencies you can trade.

---

## Available Networks

Achylo Protocol operates on two environments depending on your needs:

| Environment | Network | Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Mainnet** | Base (chain ID `8453`) | Real trades with real USDC |
| **Testnet** | Base Sepolia (chain ID `84532`) | Testing and development with mock USDC |

<Warning>
  Always verify which network is currently active before sending funds. Check the official [Achylo announcements](https://achylo.com) for the latest network status. Sending funds to the wrong network may result in loss of assets.
</Warning>

## Accessing the App

Follow these steps to go from a blank browser tab to an active P2P session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Achylo app">
    Navigate to [www.achylo.com](https://achylo.com) in your browser. The app runs entirely in your browser — no desktop client or browser extension is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Click the **Connect** button in the top navigation. 
    
    - **Returning users:** Select your existing wallet or provider.
    - **First-time users:** Sign in with your preferred **email address**. Achylo will automatically create a personal smart account wallet for you — no seed phrase to write down, no extension to install. Your wallet is self-custodied and only you control it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet with USDC">
    Once your wallet is created, you are ready to receive **USDC on the Base network**. Send USDC from an exchange or another wallet to your Achylo wallet address.

    <Warning>
      **Critical:** Always verify that funds are sent on the **Base network**. Sending USDC on a different network (e.g., Ethereum mainnet, Polygon) without using the multi-chain deposit feature will result in funds that cannot be accessed by the protocol.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the P2P marketplace">
    With your wallet connected and funded, go to **Tools → P2P** in the main navigation menu. The app will display your wallet address and unlock the full P2P marketplace interface.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you want to deposit from another blockchain (Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, etc.), use the **Multi-Chain Deposit** feature described below — it handles bridging automatically.
</Tip>

## Multi-Chain Deposits

You do not need to already hold USDC on Base to get started. Achylo supports **multi-chain deposits**, allowing you to send tokens from several popular blockchains and receive them automatically as **USDC on Base**.

### How It Works

1. **Choose your source chain** — select from the supported networks listed below.
2. **Send your tokens** — you can send native tokens (ETH, POL, BNB, AVAX) or stablecoins (USDC, USDT, DAI) from the source chain.
3. **Automatic bridging and conversion** — Achylo routes your deposit through the optimal bridge and swap path. Your funds arrive as USDC in your Achylo wallet on Base.
4. **Unified balance** — all your capital consolidates into a single USDC balance on Base, ready to use in the protocol.

### Supported Chains for Deposit

| Chain | Accepted Tokens |
|---|---|
| **Ethereum** | ETH, USDC, USDT, DAI |
| **Arbitrum** | ETH, USDC, USDT |
| **Optimism** | ETH, USDC |
| **Polygon** | POL, USDC, USDT |
| **BSC** | BNB, USDT, BUSD |
| **Avalanche** | AVAX, USDC |

<Note>
  The full list of supported chains and tokens is updated dynamically. Check the app's deposit interface for the most current options.
</Note>

### Benefits of Multi-Chain Deposits

- **No manual bridging:** No need to visit separate bridge platforms or manage multiple wallets.
- **Gas optimization:** The protocol automatically selects the most cost-efficient route.
- **Unified capital:** One USDC balance on Base — simple, clean, ready to trade.
- **Global access:** Users from any major blockchain ecosystem can participate without friction.
