Achylo's P2P module is designed for high-trust, high-reliability trading. Two systems work together to keep it that way: **transaction limits** prevent abuse and keep gas sponsorship sustainable, while **protocol fees** fund the gas sponsorship pool that lets you trade without ever touching ETH. This page explains both in full.

---

## Transaction Limits

Every wallet interacting with Achylo is subject to rate controls that prevent spam, bot abuse, and accidental gas drain. These controls operate at multiple layers simultaneously.

### Anti-Abuse Protection Layers

Three automatic protections apply to all users regardless of tier:

| Protection | Trigger | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **Anti-burst** | More than 3 transactions in any 10-second window | 30-second automatic cooldown |
| **Duplicate detection** | The same action repeated within 5 seconds | Immediate block until the 5-second window passes |
| **Progressive backoff** | 3 consecutive failed transactions | Exponential wait time, capped at a maximum of 2 minutes |

<Note>
  Even Gold users — who have unlimited daily and hourly quotas — are still subject to the anti-burst, duplicate detection, and progressive backoff protections. These are system-wide safety rails, not user-level restrictions.
</Note>

### Tier-Based Quotas

In addition to the instant protections above, each user tier has its own hourly and daily quota for on-chain actions that consume the rate limit.

| User Level | Requirements | Hourly Limit | Daily Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New** | 0 completed escrows | 10 transactions | 24 transactions |
| **Active** | 1 or more completed escrows | 20 transactions | 48 transactions |
| **Merchant** | Registered + active collateral | 30 transactions | 300 transactions |
| **Gold** | Verified account + Gold benefits | Unlimited | Unlimited |

<Tip>
  The fastest way to move from New to Active is to complete your first escrow — even a small one. From Active to Merchant, register via the [Merchant Role](/p2p/merchant) page and deposit collateral.
</Tip>

### Actions That Do NOT Count Against Your Quota

The following actions are always permitted and never consume your hourly or daily quota, regardless of your tier:

- Creating a private escrow
- Accepting a public order
- Releasing funds
- Confirming payment
- Claiming funds (reclaim timeout)
- Initiating a dispute
- Voting in a dispute

<Info>
  These exemptions ensure that the core trading lifecycle — the actions that move value between parties — is never blocked by rate limits, even if you have hit your quota for other actions.
</Info>

### Tips for Managing Your Limits

1. **Plan bulk operations** — if you need to perform multiple similar actions, space them out to avoid triggering the anti-burst protection.
2. **Avoid automation scripts** — bots that fire multiple identical transactions within seconds will be blocked by duplicate detection and backoff, defeating their own purpose.
3. **Monitor your tier** — if you consistently hit your hourly limit, consider completing more escrows to reach Active status, or registering as a Merchant for a significantly higher quota.

## Protocol Fees

Achylo sponsors all on-chain gas costs for you — you never need to hold ETH to trade. Protocol fees are the mechanism that makes this possible: a small USDC amount is collected at the moment a trade completes, covering the gas Achylo paid on your behalf throughout the escrow lifecycle.

### What Fees Are Used For

- Paying on-chain gas when the protocol signs sponsored actions on your behalf
- Maintaining and monitoring the gas pool to guarantee availability at all times
- Sustaining the Transaction Guard and other anti-abuse infrastructure

### When Fees Are Charged

The escrow contract collects the fee **once**, at the point of successful completion. There are only two moments this can happen:

1. **When the seller releases funds** — the fee is deducted from the escrow balance before the net USDC is sent to the buyer.
2. **After dispute resolution** — if an order enters dispute, the fee is deducted from the resolved amount before it is sent to the winner.

The following actions are **never** charged a fee by the escrow contract:

- Creating a public or private order
- Requesting a cancellation
- Registering as a Merchant
- Accepting a public order (fee is charged only at release)

### Who Pays

The user who executes the fee-triggering action — typically the seller clicking "Release Funds" — is the one from whose escrow balance the fee is deducted. The buyer receives the net amount after the fee has been subtracted.

### Gold Users: Zero Fees

<Info>
  **Gold users pay 0% in protocol fees.** If you hold Gold status, the escrow contract recognises your account and skips the fee deduction entirely. You can obtain Gold status by purchasing a Gold membership or through the monthly referral programme.
</Info>

When you are Gold, every modal that would normally show a fee instead displays the **⭐ Gold User — No protocol fees** badge. You save the fee on every single trade.

### Fee Structure

Fees follow a two-tier structure based on order size:

| Order Size | Fee Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 100 USDC | Fixed flat fee | 0.1 USDC |
| 100 USDC or more | Percentage of order value | 0.1% of the escrow amount |

<Note>
  The exact fee values are defined by Achylo's governance (composed of Gold users, stakers, and token holders) and can change over time. Always verify the current fee before executing a large trade.
</Note>

**To check the current fee:** navigate to **Stats → Protocol → P2P Escrow Protocol** and look at the **Fixed Fee** and **Percentage Fee** fields.

### CUBT Currency: 50% Fee Discount

If you are trading using **CUBT** as the fiat currency on any order, Achylo automatically applies a **50% discount** on the P2P protocol fee at the time of settlement. This discount is calculated and shown in the fee summary modal before you confirm.

### How Fees Are Displayed in the UI

Before you confirm any fee-triggering action, Achylo shows you the exact cost:

- **Modal summary** — each action modal (Accept Order, Release Funds, etc.) includes a breakdown showing the gross escrow amount, the fee, and the net amount the buyer will receive.
- **Tooltips** — hover over the fee line for an explanation of where the fee goes and how it was calculated.
- **Gold badge** — if you are a Gold user, the badge replaces the fee line entirely to confirm you are exempt.

### Quick Reference

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who sets the fee rate? | Achylo governance (Gold users, stakers, and token holders). Published on the Stats page. |
| Who pays the fee? | The user executing the fee-triggering action (typically the seller). |
| What is the fee used for? | Covering on-chain gas costs and maintaining the gas sponsorship pool. |
| When is the fee charged? | At the moment funds are released or after dispute resolution — once per escrow. |
| How can I avoid the fee? | Hold Gold status. CUBT orders receive a 50% discount. |
| Where do I check the current rate? | Stats → Protocol → P2P Escrow Protocol |

<Warning>
  Always review the fee summary in the modal before confirming a release or accepting a large order. Fee rates are governed on-chain and can be updated; what applied last week may differ from today's rate.
</Warning>
