This policy describes how Achylo Labs ("Achylo", "we", "us") collects, uses, retains, and protects information obtained when users interact with the dApp, smart contracts, and complementary protocol services. By using the platform, you accept the practices described in this document.

<Note>
  Last updated: **November 14, 2025**
</Note>

---

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all features of **Achylo Protocol**, including:

- P2P escrow transactions.
- CUBT token minting and redemption.
- Authentication via smart accounts.
- On-ramp and off-ramp integrations.
- Administrative tools and support interfaces.

The blockchain networks used (for example, Base L2) also form part of the ecosystem covered by this policy, as publicly recorded on-chain data is inherently accessible to all network participants.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 On-Chain Data

- Wallet addresses and smart contract events associated with your activity.
- Public transaction history executed at our contract addresses.
- References to orders, escrows, or disputes recorded on-chain.

### 2.2 Technical and Usage Data

- IP address, browser type, and operating system.
- Server logs, performance metrics, and error reports.
- Interface interaction events (for example, order creation, receipt uploads, authentication attempts).

### 2.3 Escrow and Operations Data

- Internal operation identifiers and flow statuses (created, funded, released, disputed, etc.).
- Transaction amounts, currencies, and payment methods selected by the parties.
- Minimum metadata required to execute dispute resolutions or send notifications.

### 2.4 Third-Party Integrations

When you use external services (such as Coinbase on-ramp, RPC providers, hosting infrastructure, analytics tools, or push notification services), those third parties may collect information under their own privacy policies. Achylo shares only the data strictly necessary to operate the integration.

---

## 3. Purposes of Processing

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

1. **Service operation:** Operate, maintain, and improve the dApp and smart contracts.
2. **Escrow facilitation:** Process escrow transactions and record compliance states.
3. **Authentication and recovery:** Enable authentication and recovery flows through smart accounts.
4. **On-ramp / off-ramp:** Execute fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat processes, and send related notifications.
5. **Fraud prevention:** Detect and mitigate fraud, system abuse, and automated attacks.
6. **Legal compliance:** Meet legal, regulatory, or governmental authority obligations.
7. **Analytics and optimization:** Analyze usage metrics to improve platform performance and user experience.

---

## 4. Authentication via Smart Accounts

- We may store session identifiers, temporary tokens, or hashes related to the authentication flow.
- Failed authentication attempts are logged to detect abuse and malicious automation.
- **We never store or request private keys.** Custody remains under the control of the user or the wallet provider (e.g., Coinbase Smart Wallet).

---

## 5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Achylo uses session cookies, local storage (`localStorage` / `sessionStorage`), and analytics tools to:

- Maintain active sessions and remember basic user preferences.
- Measure feature usage, performance metrics, and error rates.
- Optimize load times through local caching of configurations.

You may disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect critical functionality such as continuous authentication or access to notifications.

---

## 6. Information Sharing

**We do not sell personal information.** We may share data only with:

| Recipient | Basis for Sharing |
|---|---|
| **Infrastructure providers** | Cloud services, content delivery networks, RPC providers, IPFS storage |
| **On-ramp / off-ramp partners** | Financial entities processing requests initiated by the user |
| **Regulatory authorities or law enforcement** | When required by law or valid court orders |

In each case, we apply contractual and technical controls to limit use of information to the agreed purpose.

---

## 7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures, including:

- **Encryption in transit** via HTTPS for all data transfers.
- **Role-based access control** to limit internal data access.
- **Continuous monitoring** and audit logs of system events.
- **Periodic security reviews** and third-party assessments.

No system is entirely invulnerable. By using the platform, you accept the inherent risks of transmitting data over the internet and public blockchain networks.

---

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to:

- Provide the services you have requested.
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
- Resolve disputes or pending claims.
- Detect and mitigate recurring fraud or abuse.

Once these purposes are fulfilled, we delete or anonymize records in accordance with best practices achievable within a decentralized context. Note that on-chain data recorded on public blockchains is permanent by nature and cannot be deleted.

---

## 9. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction — including but not limited to the **LGPD** (Brazil), the **GDPR** (European Union), and other applicable local laws — you may have the right to:

- **Access and portability:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification:** Correct or update inaccurate data.
- **Deletion:** Request erasure of data where applicable and technically feasible.
- **Objection or restriction:** Object to or limit certain types of processing.

To exercise these rights, write to **[contact@achylo.com](mailto:contact@achylo.com)** detailing your request and the information necessary to verify your identity. We will respond within the timeframes required by the applicable regulation.
